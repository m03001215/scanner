Version 6 keeps v1's call exactly as it is and adds a second model that scores how likely the call is to be right, from v1's own outputs, the 18 TA votes and pre-candle regime features (trend persistence, run length, whether the call is contrarian, volatility state, position in range, v1's recent misses, session). It is trained only on v1's out-of-sample calls, walk-forward. Decision = act when the score is in the top 20% of scores seen out of sample. Times in Eastern Time.
